How it works

Fair line. Pinnacle's prices are de-vigged into fair win probabilities. If Pinnacle has no line, the median across at least 3 books is used.

market_ev bets the best US-book price whenever its expected value against the fair line is at least 2.5%. No AI.

ai_analyst bets moneylines when its adjusted probability gives at least 3% EV at the best price. It never writes or sees a price.

baseline_fav and baseline_random are control groups: always the favorite, and a coin flip.

CLV (closing line value) compares the price we got with the fair price at the evening snapshot. Positive CLV over hundreds of bets is the clearest sign of real skill.

Each version is frozen until it reaches 300 graded bets. 100 bets carry about ±9.5% ROI noise.

Paper bets only. No real money. Data: The Odds API, ESPN. AI: Gemini Flash.
