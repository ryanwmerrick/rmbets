How it works

Market EV bets when a sportsbook's price beats the sharp market's fair odds by 2.5% or more. No AI.

AI analyst reads ESPN injuries, records and rest, nudges the market's odds, and bets when it sees a 3% edge.

Favorite and Coin flip are control groups to compare against.

CLV shows whether we got a better price than where the line closed. Positive CLV over time is the best sign of real skill.

Paper bets only. Odds: The Odds API. Stats: ESPN. AI: Gemini.
