How it works

AI analyst

Reads today's injury reports, records and rest from ESPN, adjusts the market's odds by up to 5 points, and bets moneylines when it sees an edge of 3% or more.

Market EV

No AI. Compares every sportsbook to the sharpest market (Pinnacle) and bets prices that are at least 2.5% too generous, on moneylines, spreads and totals.

Favorite

Control group. Always bets the favorite to win.

Coin flip

Control group. Bets a random side. Any real strategy should beat this.

Every day

  1. ~10 AM ETGrade last night's games, pull fresh odds, place new bets.
  2. ~5 PM ETCheck the odds again before games start to measure CLV. NFL Sundays get an extra check before the 1 PM kickoffs.
  3. Next morningResults post and profits update.

Terms

Moneyline
Bet on who wins the game.
Spread
Bet on the margin. "+6.5" means that team can lose by up to 6 and still win the bet.
Total
Bet on whether the combined score goes over or under a number.
Edge (EV)
Expected Value: how much better our price is than the fair odds, as a percent.
ROI
Return on Investment: profit as a share of everything bet.
CLV
Closing Line Value: did we get a better price than where the odds ended up right before the game? Positive CLV over time is the best sign of real skill.
