Problem & Philosophy:

I wanted to make an AI model to predict sports results and find favorable odds in betting markets. However, I knew that I wasn't going to beat the $150 billion sports betting industry's models. Their resources will always lead to better raw predictions. However, traditional models often fail to account for qualitative factors (like injuries, coaching changes, and team motivation). That's why research often times increases success in sports betting. And I happen to know someone who is really good at this type of research ... AI!

Strategy:

This project leverages a multi-layered AI Agent pipeline to act as a sophisticated research assistant. The strategy focuses on finding Positive Expected Value (+EV) by identifying markets where the odds misprice the actual risk.

The Outcome:

This approach does not guarantee a high win rate; instead, it prioritizes maximizing long-term profit. By consistently exploiting mispriced odds (the 'edge'), the system is mathematically positioned to generate superior returns over the long run.

Model Version History

v1.0 Used Gemini-2.0-Flash for all stages of analysis. Optimized for speed.

v1.1 Uses Gemini-2.0-Flash for high-volume data research, then routes to Gemini-2.5-Pro for high-level reasoning and final betting decisions.

v1.2 Models are now less generous with +EV calls on Total Points markets.

v1.3 Model now accounts more for Win Probability, not just +EV, weighing more heavily momentum, recent form (last 10 games), and key injuries.
